Terms of Service

Last Updated: February 5, 2026

Purpose

The Filipino Christian Fellowship of Toronto ("New Horizons Community") operates internal digital tools for church administration, including donation tracking, offering management, and financial reporting. These tools are not commercial products and are intended solely for authorized church staff.

Authorized Use

Access to church administration tools is available exclusively to authorized staff of the Filipino Christian Fellowship of Toronto. Authorized users include the church treasurer, financial secretary, and other personnel designated by church leadership.

Acceptable Use

Users agree to:

  • Use these tools only for legitimate church financial administration
  • Keep login credentials and access tokens confidential
  • Report any suspected unauthorized access to church leadership immediately
  • Handle donor and financial information with care and confidentiality

Third-Party Integrations

Our tools may connect to third-party services such as QuickBooks Online and Gmail for the purpose of managing financial records. These integrations use OAuth 2.0 for secure authorization and can be revoked at any time by church administrators. Use of third-party services is subject to their respective terms and privacy policies.

Data Accuracy

Users are responsible for verifying the accuracy of donation and financial records. Our tools provide assistance with data entry, matching, and reconciliation, but final verification remains the responsibility of authorized staff.

Limitation of Liability

These tools are provided "as is" for internal church use. The Filipino Christian Fellowship of Toronto is not liable for any errors, data loss, or discrepancies arising from the use of these tools. Users should maintain independent records as backup in accordance with standard accounting practices.

Changes

These terms may be updated as needed. Authorized users will be notified of any changes by church leadership.
